# Store Information
**Bookstore Name:** PBA Bookstore

## About the Store
We are a modern, cozy online bookstore designed to feel like a digital Barnes & Noble. Our goal is to make discovering and shopping for books feel welcoming, relaxed, and easy to navigate.

## Homepage & Browsing
When you visit the homepage, you can explore the catalog in a few simple ways:
* **Featured & Recommended Books:** Curated picks and popular reads highlighted right up front.
* **Book Categories:** Books organized by genre and topic so you can browse by mood or interest.
* **Search Bar:** A quick search tool at the top of the page to look up specific titles, authors, or keywords.

## AI Book Assistant
Need help deciding what to read next? You can open the **AI Chatbot widget** directly on the site at any time. The assistant can:
* Recommend books based on your tastes, favorite genres, or what you're in the mood for.
* Help you find specific titles in our catalog.
* Answer general questions about the store and how to browse.
