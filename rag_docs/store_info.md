# Store RAG Documentation: "Whatever Bookstore"

## Identifiers & Overview:
**Bookstore Name:** Whatever Name
**Concept:** A general-interest independent bookstore offering fiction, non-fiction, children's books, and local authors, all housed within an immersive indoor botanical greenhouse.
**Setting:** Brooklyn, New York

## Operational Rules/ Policies: 
**Return & Exchange Policy:** 
- Books and physical merchandise may be returned within **14 days** of purchase with an original receipt. Items must be in pristine, unread condition. 
- Live plants, terrariums, and clearance items are **final sale** and cannot be returned. 

**Pricing & Discounts:** 
- Standard retail pricing aligns with publisher list prices. 
- Used and pre-loved book trade-ins grant store credit valued at 30% of the book's resale price. 

**Membership Perks ("Leaf & Page Club"):** 
- Members pay a $10 monthly subscription fee. 
- Perks include a 15% discount on all book purchases, a free cutting from the in-store greenhouse every month, and free admission to weekend author readings and poetry slams.
